# Sharing courses

## Getting a course (learners)

When someone sends you a course, it waits for you under **Invitations** on your Courses page. It says who sent it and shows their note.

- **Accept:** the course appears in your courses and downloads so it works offline. You'll see a progress bar. If the download is interrupted, press **Download again**.
- **Decline:** the course goes away, and the sender sees that you declined.

If Dayspring runs on your computer, it tells you about the invitation too, and you can say "accept the course".

**Other ways in**
- **An invite code:** paste it under **Have an invite code?**. The course then waits under Invitations.
- **More courses:** titles the owner has listed. Press **Request** and the owner can approve it with one click.
- **A course file** (`.lpack`): **Add a course from a file**.

**Your progress**
- Everything is saved on your computer first, as you go, so the app works with no internet at all.
- When you're online it syncs to your account, so a second computer picks up where you left off.
- If both computers were offline, both sets of work are merged: nothing finished is ever un-finished, and your best score counts.

## Friends first

Courses go to your **friends**. It's the simplest way to know who's who.

1. **They install Lantern and sign up** to your hub (the same Project URL and key you use). They appear under **Friends → People on your hub**, and under **Owner → People**, with whether they're online and their Lantern version.
2. **Send a friend request.** On **Friends**, click **Add friend** next to them. Or type their **friend code** (like `LNT-7K3Q`, shown at the top of their Friends page) or their **email address** under **Add a friend**.
3. **They answer.** Their Lantern shows the request straight away (and Dayspring announces it, if they use it). They can **Accept**, **Decline** or **Ignore**. Ignore puts it under *Set aside*, where they can still accept it later; you just see "Waiting".
4. **Send the course.** Once you're friends, click **Send a course** next to them on **Friends**, pick the course and add a note. They get an invitation to **Accept**; the course then appears in their Courses and downloads for offline use.

**Someone without Lantern yet?** Type their email address under **Add a friend** and send the request anyway. It waits for them and shows up when they sign up, in Lantern or in Dayspring's "Connect to Lantern". Dayspring then offers to install Lantern for them.

**Good to know**
- Only the owner of the hub sends courses. Anyone can have friends.
- People find you by your exact friend code or email. Turn on **Let people find me by my name** on the Friends page if you want name search too.
- **Block** someone and they can't send you requests or find you. Unblock them under **Friends → Blocked**.
- At most 20 friend requests a day each, and after a "no" the same person can be asked again after a week.
- The owner can still send a course to anyone from **Owner → People** (for example, a whole group); friends are just the everyday way.

## Sending a course (the owner)

**Owner → People** lists everyone with Lantern on your hub: who's online now, their Lantern version, what they're studying, and their progress.

**Send to one or many**
1. Tick people, or tick the box at the top to select everyone.
2. Click **Send course…**.
3. Pick the course and write a short note if you like.
4. To reach people who haven't signed up yet, add their **email addresses**. The offer waits for them and attaches when they sign up with that email.
5. Click **Send**. People who already have the course, or already have an offer waiting, are skipped.

**Tracking**
- **Owner → Offers** shows every offer as pending, accepted, declined or withdrawn. You can **Withdraw** a pending one.
- **Owner → Courses** shows, for each course: who has it and their progress, who hasn't answered yet, and the course's settings.

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| Status | **Draft** (only you see it) or **Published** |
| Who can open it | **Only people I send it to**, or **Everyone signed in** |
| **Listed** | Everyone can see the title under "More courses" and ask for it |
| **Make an invite code** | A code anyone can use for 30 days, for people you'd rather not add by email |

**Groups**
- **Owner → Groups:** make a group (e.g. "Spring cohort") and add courses and people.
- Everyone in the group is offered the group's courses, including people you add later.

**Requests**
- **Owner → Requests:** people asking for a listed course, with their note. **Approve** gives them the course right away.

**You always have every course** yourself, with no offer needed.

## Updating a course

- **You:** publish a new version (see the course authoring guide).
- **Everyone who has it:** gets it on their next sync and sees "*… was updated. Your progress is kept.*" What changed is under **What's new**.
