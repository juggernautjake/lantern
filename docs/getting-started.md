# Getting started

1. **Install Lantern:** see [Installing Lantern](install.md).
2. **Open it** from the desktop icon. It opens in your browser, and runs only on your computer.
3. **Get a course:**
   - **Someone is sharing courses with you:** go to **Settings → Account & hub**, paste the hub address and public key they gave you, and create your account. Their course is waiting under **Invitations**; press **Accept**.
   - **You have a course file** (`.lpack`): on the Courses page, choose **Add a course from a file**.
4. **Open the course.** Its page lists every unit, lesson, exercise, project and check, with what you've done, what's next and about how long is left. Press **Start** or **Continue**, or click any lesson.
5. **Study.** Everything saves as you go. Go back to the overview with **← Overview** at the top.
6. **Ask Lantern.** The glowing lantern beside the course is your study companion: "give me a hint", "what's next?", "find me a video about this", "play some focus music". Type, press 🎙️, or say "Lantern, …". See [Lantern, your study companion](assistant.md).

**Around the app**

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **What's new** | Every update to Lantern and to your courses |
| **Settings** | Your account, the assistant (its AI, voice, sound and look), reminders, colours (evening or day, high contrast), display size, updates, backups and Dayspring |
| **🔊** and **Lantern** (top right) | The sound panel, and showing or hiding the assistant |
| **Owner** | Only for the person who runs the hub; see [Sharing courses](sharing-courses.md) |

**Running your own hub** so you can share courses with others: [Setting up your hub](setup-supabase.md).
