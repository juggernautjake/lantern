# Questions

**Does Lantern need the internet?**
Only to receive courses, to sync, and to update. Once a course is downloaded, everything in it works offline, including exercises, grading and projects.

**Is my information shared?**
Only with the hub you connect to (the course owner's): your name, email, which courses you have, and your progress. That's what lets them see how you're doing. Nothing else leaves your computer. Lantern listens only on your own computer.

**I have two computers.**
Sign in on both. Your progress follows you. If you studied on both while offline, the work is merged: finished stays finished, and your best score counts.

**I used the web version of a course. Can I keep my progress?**
Yes. On the web version, open **Progress**, press **Make a backup** and copy all the text. In Lantern, open the course, press **Bring your progress from the web version…** and paste it. Nothing you did in Lantern is lost.

**Someone sent me a course but I don't see it.**
- Check that you're signed in (Settings → Account & hub) with the **same email** they sent it to.
- Press **Sync now**.
- Look under **Invitations** on the Courses page.

**The course didn't download.**
Press **Download again** on its card. Downloads resume on the next sync if you were offline.

**The sign-in email never came.**
Free hubs can send only a few emails an hour. Wait a little, check your spam folder, or ask the owner to allow password sign-up (see the hub setup guide).

**How do I stop Lantern?**
Start menu → Lantern → **Stop Lantern**. Everything is already saved.

**An update went wrong.**
Lantern puts the old version back by itself and tells you. Your data was backed up before it started, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Lantern\backups`.

**Does it work with Dayspring?**
Yes. When both are on your computer:
- Dayspring can tell you about course invitations, show your progress, open your next lesson, and add study times to your schedule.
- Lantern stays quiet during Dayspring's quiet hours and calls.

Each works fully without the other.

**Can I make my own course?**
Yes. See `docs/dev/authoring-courses.md`. A course is a folder, and one command builds and publishes it.
